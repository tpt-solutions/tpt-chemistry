## [0.1.0] - not yet released

### Added

- Activity-coefficient models: Debye–Hückel (limiting and extended), Davies, Pitzer and single-solvent eNRTL.
- Solubility products and saturation indices.
- Calcium-phosphate chemistry and chelator-controlled precipitation onset (the "chalkiness" model).
- Kinetic nucleation onset, with the nucleation-rate floor documented.

### Changed

- Doc comments state the nucleation-rate floor value explicitly.
