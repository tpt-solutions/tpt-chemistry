tpt-chemistry Design Specification (spec.txt)
1. Overview
Name: tpt-chemistry
Description: A pure-Rust, AI-native computational chemistry and molecular simulation library. It provides molecular dynamics (MD), quantum chemistry primitives (Hartree-Fock/DFT), reaction kinetics, and crystallography without C/C++ FFI or external solver dependencies.
License: MIT (all dependencies must be MIT or compatible; no Apache-2.0-only crates).
Core Philosophy: Unit-safe, no_std + alloc compatible at the core, differentiable for machine-learned potentials, and mathematically rigorous via native Rust verification tooling and from-scratch implementations.
2. Design Principles
Zero External Solver Dependencies: No LAMMPS FFI, no Gaussian, no Quantum ESPRESSO. All algorithms (SCF iteration, electron repulsion integrals, Ewald summation, neighbor lists) are implemented from scratch in pure Rust, leveraging tpt-math for linear algebra and ODE solving.
Strict Licensing: No Apache-2.0-only dependencies. The workspace strictly uses MIT, MIT/Apache-2.0 dual, or custom in-house crates.
Type-State & Dimensional Safety: Compile-time prevention of unit and coordinate mismatches. Phantom types distinguish between Cartesian and internal (Z-matrix) coordinates. All physical quantities (distance in Angstroms/Bohr, time in femtoseconds, energy in Hartrees/kJ/mol) are wrapped in tpt-math-units.
Native Formal Verification: Safety and physical invariants enforced via:
Kani Rust Verifier: For bounded model checking of critical loops (e.g., proving neighbor-list cell indexing is always in-bounds, or that SCF iteration density matrices remain positive semi-definite).
Property-Based Testing (proptest): For statistical verification of physical laws (e.g., "Velocity Verlet integration conserves total energy within a bounded tolerance", "mass is strictly conserved in closed reaction networks").

4. Crate Specifications
tpt-chem-core
Purpose: The foundational layer. Defines molecular topology, atom/bond types, and force field parameterization.
Dependencies: tpt-math (internal, MIT).
Key Features:
Graph-based molecular representation with const-generic atom types.
Force field definitions (Lennard-Jones, Coulomb, harmonic bonds/angles, Ryckaert-Bellemans dihedrals).
NIST physical constants and unit conversions.
no_std + alloc compatible.
tpt-chem-md
Purpose: Classical molecular dynamics engine for simulating large ensembles of atoms.
Key Features:
Symplectic integrators: Velocity Verlet, Leapfrog.
Thermostats and Barostats: Langevin, Nosé-Hoover, Berendsen.
Spatial partitioning: From-scratch cell lists and Verlet neighbor lists for

Long-range interactions: Ewald summation and Particle Mesh Ewald (PME) for electrostatics (using tpt-dsp for 3D FFTs).
tpt-chem-quantum
Purpose: Electronic structure primitives for ab initio and semi-empirical quantum chemistry.
Key Features:
Gaussian-type orbital (GTO) basis set management (STO-nG, Pople, Dunning sets).
From-scratch evaluation of multi-center electron repulsion integrals (ERIs) using the Obara-Saika or McMurchie-Davidson schemes.
Self-Consistent Field (SCF) iteration, density mixing, and Hartree-Fock (HF) matrix construction.
Relies on tpt-math for dense eigenvalue decomposition (solving the Roothaan-Hall equations).
tpt-chem-kinetics
Purpose: Simulation of chemical reaction networks and population dynamics.
Key Features:
Deterministic mass-action kinetics (stiff ODE systems, leveraging tpt-math solvers).
Stochastic Simulation Algorithm (SSA / Gillespie algorithm) for low-copy-number regimes.
Temperature-dependent rate constants (Arrhenius, Eyring equations).
Equilibrium and steady-state analysis.
tpt-chem-crystal
Purpose: Solid-state physics and crystallography primitives.
Key Features:
Bravais lattice generation, Miller indices, and Wigner-Seitz cell construction.
Space group symmetry operations and Wyckoff positions.
Reciprocal lattice generation and Brillouin zone sampling (k-point grids).
X-ray diffraction (XRD) pattern simulation from structure factors.
tpt-chem-io
Purpose: Interoperability with standard chemical file formats.
Key Features:
Parsers and writers for XYZ, PDB, MOL2, and CIF (Crystallographic Information File).
Trajectory streaming for large MD outputs.

5. Integration & Synergies
tpt-materials: tpt-chemistry provides the bottom-up micro-scale simulations (MD, DFT) that feed homogenized macro-scale properties (elastic constants, thermal conductivity) into tpt-materials.
tpt-math: Provides the heavy linear algebra (eigen-solvers for HF, sparse matrix operations for Hamiltonians) and ODE solvers for kinetics.
tpt-dsp: Provides the 3D FFTs required for Particle Mesh Ewald (PME) in MD, and FFTs of velocity autocorrelation functions to compute vibrational density of states (phonon spectra).
tpt-fem / tpt-physics: Enables QM/MM (Quantum Mechanics / Molecular Mechanics) coupling, where the quantum region is embedded in a continuum FEM or CFD environment.
AI-Native Agents: Molecular dynamics forces and quantum energy surfaces expose automatic differentiation hooks. This allows tpt-eve / tpt-anima agents to train machine-learned interatomic potentials (MLIPs) or perform differentiable molecular design.
